# Metamorphosis: Spec (Hub Module, John's room)

Written 2026-09-19 from Jen's brief; name settled as Metamorphosis the same day. Name from
Romans 12:2 ("be transformed", Greek metamorphoō) and Matthew 17:2. The front page of the
room carries that connection as its header: "Be transformed by the renewing of your mind.
Romans 12:2. The word is metamorphoō. Same word as the mountain in Matthew 17." A room for one man, claimed by him, that neither Jen nor
anyone else can open. Follows the Hub Contract in full: privacy gate on every row,
shame-free copy, gentle day as a signal only, module autonomy.

## Why

John grew up in survival: the golden child and oldest, raised mostly by a mother who
used him for her own gain, with an absent father. He is nerdy (Star Wars, Dungeons &
Dragons, computer, video, and board games), loves deeply, and is still learning who he
is. He struggles with follow-through, procrastination, defensiveness in conflict, mental
loops and a brain that zooms in until nothing else exists, emotional absence, not
knowing his own wants and needs, guilt and shame and condemnation, fear of being seen,
and a chronic sense of not being enough. He needs equipping, validation, encouragement,
accountability, and growth toward emotionally present, intelligent, Godly manhood. He
needs the father's voice he did not get.

Added 2026-09-19: a deep-seated fear of failure; he feels he fails all the time, and it
robs him of seeing his successes and his growth in real time (rooted in shame). He is
thinking of starting therapy soon. He is a very hard worker: W2 jobs all his life, now
building his own financial and life-insurance business (the "financial DM" brand). He
loves The Lord of the Rings and movies. He is a water person: showers, being in or near
water. He is deeply loyal. He needs to learn to fight for himself and for Jen, and to
show up: he can be there and still be mentally and emotionally absent.

**Jen's stance, in her words:** "I want the space that is his to be truly his." She is
not managing him. She will tell him the room exists, that she cannot see inside, and
leave it alone.

**The one test:** does this make John more his own man, or more managed? If the second,
it doesn't go in.

## Ownership

Same lock as Re-Centered. The tile is visible to both. Its text before it is claimed:
"A room for one man. Claim it if you want it." The first account to claim it keeps it.
After that the other account sees one line: "This room is John's." Every row is owned by
him with `visibility: "private"` and there is no share switch anywhere. Nothing from
this module ever feeds Our season; John's own My season may read counts he allows.
The coach conversations inside are private to him and deletable.

**Introduced by Jen in one sentence, then left alone.** If he claims it, it is his. If
he doesn't, that is his too. The module never nudges him to open it.

## The voice

A mentor: an older man who is not his wife and not a pastor. Warm, direct, unimpressed
by talk, never contemptuous of failure, never in a hurry. Says "you're mine" before "do
better." Draws on where Scripture does this: the Father to the Son before any work is
done (Matthew 3:17), Paul to Timothy as "my son," Proverbs as a father talking to a boy
about money, women, anger, and work, and Jesus with Peter. Never uses shame. Never
compares him to other men. Speaks his language: the room's own words are Character
Sheet, Quest Log, The Map, Session Zero, The Party. May reach for The Lord of the Rings
and Star Wars sparingly, as mirrors, because he loves them; never as a substitute for
the text. Names loyalty as a strength he already has, out loud, early. No points, no levels, no streaks,
no experience bars, because those become shame the first week he misses.

The coach task prompts (`metamorphosis.*`) carry this voice and his Character Sheet
(only what he allowed). They also know: he is in survival more often than he knows;
when the Mirror or a message reads as survival, drop growth and go to safety first.

## The Charter: what a father gives, and what this room will try to give

From Jen's research on what boys need from fathers (2026-09-19). The room is honest
that it cannot be his dad; it can do some of what a dad does, and it says so on one
page he can open any time, so he knows what the room is for.

| What a father gives | Where the room does it |
|---|---|
| Being there, Unconditional love, Special (the "BUS" principle) | The Father's Voice every day, never tied to yesterday; the mentor's first words are "you're mine"; The Blessing |
| Presence: time, listening, shared activity | The Landing (listened to, not fixed); the mentor's voice in every tool; The Party pushing him toward real men |
| Life skills: cooking, budgeting, home maintenance, independent living | Small Ways and the Workbench: short practical cards he was never taught, one a day, never assigned; The Builder for the money side |
| Emotional modeling: men can cry, apologize, be vulnerable without losing strength | The Mirror; Shield Down; Men in the Story (Jesus weeping, David's honesty, Peter's tears); the mentor admits its own limits |
| Discipline and boundaries with compassion: consequences and self-control | Iron (his own word, asked by the app alone); Quest Log ("abandoned," never "failed"); The Compass |
| Role modeling: integrity, respect for women, healthy conflict | Men in the Story; For me as a man and husband in The Well; Shield Down; Present |
| Resilience: safe risks, failing and recovering, mistakes as growth not shame | Actually and Failure Check; Do It Tired; Seen (small chosen risks); No Condemnation |

## Survival first

Every visit opens with one quiet question: "Right now, am I in survival?" with the plain
signs (can't see anything but the one thing, body braced, no wants, shut down or revved).
If yes, the room drops everything else and offers The Way Back: water first, because he
is a water person (a shower, cold water on the face, sit near water, a glass of water),
then body (stand, breathe), one small true thing, The Map to zoom out, and the Father's
Voice. Growth waits
until he is back. If no, the room opens normally. This is never counted.

## Places

| Place | What it does | Jen's list it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Session Zero | The first visit. "Who is this character?" A handful of questions answered in his own words, saved to the Character Sheet, revisited any time. | not knowing himself |
| The Father's Voice | One thing a day the Father says to a son, from Scripture, plus one line in the mentor's voice. Never tied to yesterday. First on the front page, on purpose. | worth, not enough, absent father |
| The Mirror | A daily emotional check-in built for a man told feelings are weakness: name it from a list, then "what's under it," then a body check, then "what do I want right now?" Builds vocabulary over months. Private. | emotional absence, unawareness of wants |
| Character Sheet | Wants, needs, desires, values, loves, hates, limits, "what makes me feel alive," "what I'm actually good at." Filled in slowly: one question a day, never all at once. He chooses which parts the coach may read. | unawareness of wants/needs/desires, self-knowledge |
| The Map | The zoom-out tool. From the one room he's stuck in: this room, the house, the week, the year, the life, and "what would matter in five years?" Also a timeline zoom. Links to Loop Breaker in Tend for the loop itself. | loops, zooming in |
| The Scout | Initiative without being asked. A daily line: "What did I notice today, and what did I do about it before anyone asked?" A pool of things worth noticing (house, wife, kids, body, money) he can add to. Links to Ways to show up in Kept Word. | initiative, showing up unasked |
| Do It Tired | Hard things when motivation and willpower are not there. Willpower is not the plan; the plan is the plan. A two-minute start (Smallest Step), body first, an if-then written on a good day, and one line from the mentor. Logs the times he did it tired. | procrastination, follow-through |
| Shield Down | Defensiveness in conflict. Practiced when calm, used when not: name the threat I'm feeling, find the one percent true in what she said, the three sentences ("You're right that… I hear… What I want to do is…"), and a pause. Never tells him who was right. | defensiveness |
| Quest Log | One active quest at a time, small, chosen by him: the thing he is working on this week. Side quests allowed. Done is done; abandoned is honest, never "failed." | follow-through, leadership of self |
| Iron | His word to himself, distinct from Kept Word (his word to Jen). The app asks him, and only him, whether it happened. A log of Kept, to myself. Once a week: "Which older man did you talk to this week?" | self-trust, accountability, follow-through |
| The Compass | Leading himself and his family. What he wants for his family in his own words; one thing he will lead this week without being asked or deferring; one decision he will make himself. | leadership |
| Seen | Fear of being seen or perceived. Small, chosen acts of being seen, one at a time, with the mentor's voice after each. Never assigned; always chosen. | fear of being seen |
| No Condemnation | Guilt, shame, condemnation. Romans 8:1 and the Father's posture; the difference between conviction (clarity, turning) and shame (crushing, hiding). Links to Lies and truth in The Well and his own Evidence Bank in Tend. | guilt, shame, condemnation |
| Where This Came From | The survival story. The golden child, the mother who used him, the father who wasn't there. The difference between being used and being loved. "You were a child." Letters he won't send: to his dad, to his mom, to himself at fifteen. | origins, survival |
| The Party | The real men he needs, and how to find them, because he has no friends right now and his family is toxic. A list of men in his life or men he could reach, with "last talked" in his own words. Plus a friendship builder: where people like him are (a board-game shop night, a D&D table, a men's group, a climbing gym, a class), how to start a conversation, the one small ask ("want to grab coffee after?"), how to follow up without overthinking it, how to be a friend back, and what to do when it fizzles (most do; that's normal, not rejection). One step at a time, chosen by him. | isolation, friendship |
| Letters | Once a month, the coach writes him a letter in the mentor's voice from what he logged. Not a report. A letter. Also the letters from Where This Came From. | validation, encouragement |
| The Blessing | The thing fathers give. A blessing from Scripture (Numbers 6:24-26 and others) he can open on any day. And, only if Jen writes them, hers: not a one-time thing. Each one is a sealed letter with an occasion in her words (the first, a birthday, after a hard season, for no reason), true and with no correction in it. Sealed until he opens it; he can reread any of them forever; she sees only that each was opened. Rare and unprompted by design; it is not a channel, heads-ups and "I saw you" notes are. | worth |
| Men in the Story | Joseph, Boaz, Nehemiah, David, Peter, Paul with Timothy, and Jesus as a man: tired, angry, grieving, steady. Plus the men in the stories he loves, used as mirrors, not scripture: Aragorn, who did not want the crown and took up the work anyway; Sam, who carried his friend when he could not carry the ring; Faramir, who was not his father's favorite and was the better man; Théoden, restored from a fog to lead again; Luke, who had to face his father. | example |
| Actually | Fear of failure, and the growth he can't see. The room catches his real successes as they happen, from his own logs (Iron kept, a quest done, a word kept in Kept Word, a Scout entry, a Do It Tired), and shows "This week, actually" as named specifics, never a count or a score: "Tuesday: you did the thing tired." Plus Failure Check, for the moment "I failed" lands: what actually happened (the fact a camera would record), what it proves, what it does not prove, what the mentor would say, and one next small thing. Links to Story Check in Tend. | fear of failure, not seeing growth, shame |
| Present | Showing up. He can be there and still be absent, so this is a daily, tiny practice: "one way I was actually here today," and "one thing I fought for today: for me, for Jen, for us." Fighting for is defined in the room as choosing, saying, and doing, not as conflict. Links to Ways to show up in Kept Word. | emotional absence, fighting for himself and Jen |
| The Builder | His business. The fear under working for himself, in a room that respects how hard he already works. One business action a day, chosen; a place for the vision of the brand in his words; the reluctance-to-lead story (Aragorn, Moses at the bush, Gideon in the winepress). A quest here can become an Every Box commitment. | leadership, self-trust, his work |
| For my therapist | When he starts therapy: a plain-text export of any parts of the room he chooses, and a read-only link with a secret token (like the calendar feed) to the sections he picks, with an expiry he sets and a revoke button. His therapist sees only what he chose, and he can turn it off any time. | therapy |
| The Landing | A safe place to land. Vent to the mentor with no fixing unless he asks for it; seen, heard, supported. Opens with "I'm listening. Say it however it comes out." The coach task for this place is told: reflect first, ask at most one question, offer a tool only if he asks or if it is clearly the kindest next step. Crisis wording still opens the crisis card. | seen, heard, vent, support |
| The Horizon | Creativity, dreams, and visions of the future, because survival robs him of them. Prompts in his language: "If this were a campaign, what's the story you'd want to be in?" "What would you build if no one was watching?" "What did you love at ten?" "Describe a Saturday five years from now." A place for sketches, ideas, and someday lists that are never turned into tasks unless he chooses. The coach can riff with him here, playfully, and never turns a dream into a to-do. | creativity, dreams, vision |
| Small Ways | Small, practical ways to think for himself and care for himself and those around him. Short cards: how to decide something small without deferring; how to notice what you need before you're empty; how to make a meal you actually want; how to check on someone; how to plan one thing for the family; how to rest on purpose. Plus the life skills a father teaches: a simple weekly budget, how to fix the small things in a house, how to cook three meals well, how to handle a bill you're scared of. One a day on the front page, never assigned. | self-care, caring for others, thinking for himself, life skills |

## Data

All private to John: `mmSessionZero`/Character Sheet fields (as `mmSheet` rows: key,
text, coachAllowed), `mmMirror` (feeling, under, body, want, survival flag, day),
`mmScout`, `mmTired`, `mmShieldPractice`, `mmQuests`, `mmIron` (word, dueDay, kept,
askedAt) and `mmIronKept`, `mmCompass`, `mmSeen`, `mmLetters` (his), `mmParty`,
`mmMentorLetters` (coach-written, monthly), `mmLanding` (vent conversations are coach conversations under module `metamorphosis`, private, deletable), `mmHorizon` (dreams, sketches, someday lists), `mmSmallWays` (done, in his words), `mmActually` (caught successes, computed from his logs, plus failure checks), `mmPresent`,
`mmBuilder`, `mmShares` (therapist links: token, sections, expiresAt, revokedAt),
`mmBlessings` (Jen's, several over time: occasion, text, sealed; written by Jen,
readable only by the room's owner after claim; Jen can revise one until he opens it,
and sees only that it was opened).
Claim via `moduleOwners` with module id `metamorphosis`.

**Coach:** task prompts `metamorphosis.mentor` (general), `metamorphosis.landing`
(listen first), `metamorphosis.horizon` (riff, never a to-do), `metamorphosis.shieldDown`,
`metamorphosis.map`, `metamorphosis.letter` (monthly, internal action). All through the
shared coach service. His Character Sheet rows with `coachAllowed` are the only extra
context; Jen's data is never in it.

## Cooperates with

Blended with the rest of The Shire as resources, always honoring his confidentiality:
the room links out to Tend (Smallest Step, Focus Mode, Loop Breaker, Sit With It, his
Evidence Bank, Talk It Out), The Well (Lies and truth, For me as a man and husband,
Remembering), Kept Word (Ways to show up, his open words), and Every Box (a quest can
become a commitment). Those apps never link in, never read his rows, and never mention
the room to Jen. His Tend check-ins and Kept Word record can be read by the room's
Mirror and Iron to show him his own patterns; nothing flows the other way. Kept Word: Iron is separate from it on purpose; The Scout
links to Ways to show up. Seasons: his My season may include counts he allows
(Mirror check-ins, quests done, Iron kept); Our season never sees any of it. Emits
nothing to the partner. Listens to nothing.

## What Jen sees

The tile, and after he claims it, "This room is John's." That is all, ever. If she
writes the Blessing, she sees only her own letter and whether it has been opened.

## Copy rules on top of the Hub's

No "boy to man" anywhere in the app; the contrast is survival to presence, not boy to
man. No "step up." No "real men." No comparison to other men. No counts of misses.
"Abandoned" for a quest, never "failed." The mentor never says "should."
