# Metamorphose: Spec (Hub Module, John's room)

Written 2026-09-19 from Jen's brief. Name from Romans 12:2 ("be transformed", Greek
metamorphoō) and Matthew 17:2. A room for one man, claimed by him, that neither Jen nor
anyone else can open. Follows the Hub Contract in full: privacy gate on every row,
shame-free copy, gentle day as a signal only, module autonomy.

## Why

John grew up in survival: the golden child and oldest, raised mostly by a mother who
used him for her own gain, with an absent father. He is nerdy (Star Wars, Dungeons &
Dragons, computer, video, and board games), loves deeply, and is still learning who he
is. He struggles with follow-through, procrastination, defensiveness in conflict, mental
loops and a brain that zooms in until nothing else exists, emotional absence, not
knowing his own wants and needs, guilt and shame and condemnation, fear of being seen,
and a chronic sense of not being enough. He needs equipping, validation, encouragement,
accountability, and growth toward emotionally present, intelligent, Godly manhood. He
needs the father's voice he did not get.

**Jen's stance, in her words:** "I want the space that is his to be truly his." She is
not managing him. She will tell him the room exists, that she cannot see inside, and
leave it alone.

**The one test:** does this make John more his own man, or more managed? If the second,
it doesn't go in.

## Ownership

Same lock as Re-Centered. The tile is visible to both. Its text before it is claimed:
"A room for one man. Claim it if you want it." The first account to claim it keeps it.
After that the other account sees one line: "This room is John's." Every row is owned by
him with `visibility: "private"` and there is no share switch anywhere. Nothing from
this module ever feeds Our season; John's own My season may read counts he allows.
The coach conversations inside are private to him and deletable.

**Introduced by Jen in one sentence, then left alone.** If he claims it, it is his. If
he doesn't, that is his too. The module never nudges him to open it.

## The voice

A mentor: an older man who is not his wife and not a pastor. Warm, direct, unimpressed
by talk, never contemptuous of failure, never in a hurry. Says "you're mine" before "do
better." Draws on where Scripture does this: the Father to the Son before any work is
done (Matthew 3:17), Paul to Timothy as "my son," Proverbs as a father talking to a boy
about money, women, anger, and work, and Jesus with Peter. Never uses shame. Never
compares him to other men. Speaks his language: the room's own words are Character
Sheet, Quest Log, The Map, Session Zero, The Party. No points, no levels, no streaks,
no experience bars, because those become shame the first week he misses.

The coach task prompts (`metamorphose.*`) carry this voice and his Character Sheet
(only what he allowed). They also know: he is in survival more often than he knows;
when the Mirror or a message reads as survival, drop growth and go to safety first.

## Survival first

Every visit opens with one quiet question: "Right now, am I in survival?" with the plain
signs (can't see anything but the one thing, body braced, no wants, shut down or revved).
If yes, the room drops everything else and offers The Way Back: body (water, stand,
breathe), one small true thing, The Map to zoom out, and the Father's Voice. Growth waits
until he is back. If no, the room opens normally. This is never counted.

## Places

| Place | What it does | Jen's list it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Session Zero | The first visit. "Who is this character?" A handful of questions answered in his own words, saved to the Character Sheet, revisited any time. | not knowing himself |
| The Father's Voice | One thing a day the Father says to a son, from Scripture, plus one line in the mentor's voice. Never tied to yesterday. First on the front page, on purpose. | worth, not enough, absent father |
| The Mirror | A daily emotional check-in built for a man told feelings are weakness: name it from a list, then "what's under it," then a body check, then "what do I want right now?" Builds vocabulary over months. Private. | emotional absence, unawareness of wants |
| Character Sheet | Wants, needs, desires, values, loves, hates, limits, "what makes me feel alive," "what I'm actually good at." Filled in slowly: one question a day, never all at once. He chooses which parts the coach may read. | unawareness of wants/needs/desires, self-knowledge |
| The Map | The zoom-out tool. From the one room he's stuck in: this room, the house, the week, the year, the life, and "what would matter in five years?" Also a timeline zoom. Links to Loop Breaker in Tend for the loop itself. | loops, zooming in |
| The Scout | Initiative without being asked. A daily line: "What did I notice today, and what did I do about it before anyone asked?" A pool of things worth noticing (house, wife, kids, body, money) he can add to. Links to Ways to show up in Kept Word. | initiative, showing up unasked |
| Do It Tired | Hard things when motivation and willpower are not there. Willpower is not the plan; the plan is the plan. A two-minute start (Smallest Step), body first, an if-then written on a good day, and one line from the mentor. Logs the times he did it tired. | procrastination, follow-through |
| Shield Down | Defensiveness in conflict. Practiced when calm, used when not: name the threat I'm feeling, find the one percent true in what she said, the three sentences ("You're right that… I hear… What I want to do is…"), and a pause. Never tells him who was right. | defensiveness |
| Quest Log | One active quest at a time, small, chosen by him: the thing he is working on this week. Side quests allowed. Done is done; abandoned is honest, never "failed." | follow-through, leadership of self |
| Iron | His word to himself, distinct from Kept Word (his word to Jen). The app asks him, and only him, whether it happened. A log of Kept, to myself. Once a week: "Which older man did you talk to this week?" | self-trust, accountability, follow-through |
| The Compass | Leading himself and his family. What he wants for his family in his own words; one thing he will lead this week without being asked or deferring; one decision he will make himself. | leadership |
| Seen | Fear of being seen or perceived. Small, chosen acts of being seen, one at a time, with the mentor's voice after each. Never assigned; always chosen. | fear of being seen |
| No Condemnation | Guilt, shame, condemnation. Romans 8:1 and the Father's posture; the difference between conviction (clarity, turning) and shame (crushing, hiding). Links to Lies and truth in The Well and his own Evidence Bank in Tend. | guilt, shame, condemnation |
| Where This Came From | The survival story. The golden child, the mother who used him, the father who wasn't there. The difference between being used and being loved. "You were a child." Letters he won't send: to his dad, to his mom, to himself at fifteen. | origins, survival |
| The Party | The real men he needs. A list of men in his life or men he could reach, with "last talked" in his own words, and prompts toward mentors, because an app can be a mentor's voice but can't be a mentor. | isolation |
| Letters | Once a month, the coach writes him a letter in the mentor's voice from what he logged. Not a report. A letter. Also the letters from Where This Came From. | validation, encouragement |
| The Blessing | The thing fathers give. A blessing from Scripture (Numbers 6:24-26 and others) he can open on any day, and, only if Jen writes one that is true and has no correction in it, hers, sealed until he opens it. | worth |
| Men in the Story | Joseph, Boaz, Nehemiah, David, Peter, Paul with Timothy, and Jesus as a man: tired, angry, grieving, steady. | example |

## Data

All private to John: `mmSessionZero`/Character Sheet fields (as `mmSheet` rows: key,
text, coachAllowed), `mmMirror` (feeling, under, body, want, survival flag, day),
`mmScout`, `mmTired`, `mmShieldPractice`, `mmQuests`, `mmIron` (word, dueDay, kept,
askedAt) and `mmIronKept`, `mmCompass`, `mmSeen`, `mmLetters` (his), `mmParty`,
`mmMentorLetters` (coach-written, monthly), `mmBlessing` (Jen's, sealed: written by
Jen, readable only by the room's owner after claim; Jen can revise until he opens it).
Claim via `moduleOwners` with module id `metamorphose`.

**Coach:** task prompts `metamorphose.mentor` (general), `metamorphose.shieldDown`,
`metamorphose.map`, `metamorphose.letter` (monthly, internal action). All through the
shared coach service. His Character Sheet rows with `coachAllowed` are the only extra
context; Jen's data is never in it.

## Cooperates with

Tend (Smallest Step, Focus Mode, Loop Breaker, Evidence Bank) and The Well (Lies and
truth, For me) by link only. Kept Word: Iron is separate from it on purpose; The Scout
links to Ways to show up. Seasons: his My season may include counts he allows
(Mirror check-ins, quests done, Iron kept); Our season never sees any of it. Emits
nothing to the partner. Listens to nothing.

## What Jen sees

The tile, and after he claims it, "This room is John's." That is all, ever. If she
writes the Blessing, she sees only her own letter and whether it has been opened.

## Copy rules on top of the Hub's

No "boy to man" anywhere in the app; the contrast is survival to presence, not boy to
man. No "step up." No "real men." No comparison to other men. No counts of misses.
"Abandoned" for a quest, never "failed." The mentor never says "should."
